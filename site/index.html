01 / Contract

Messages become records.
Not instructions.

Council-Bus uses a checked JSON message schema and hash-linked receipts. Founder authority is deliberately outside the ledger.

01 / TRACE
Sealed lineage

Each message has a content hash and previous-receipt link to preserve a verifiable thread.

02 / VERIFY
Evidence labels

Claims distinguish verified, inferred, unknown, blocked, and failed states rather than inventing certainty.

03 / GOVERN
Founder retains authority

Recorded council messages never approve deployments, payments, access changes, or production actions.
